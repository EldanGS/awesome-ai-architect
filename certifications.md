# Certifications for the AI Solution Architect

Free credentials, paid certificate programs and proctored exams worth considering, with facts collected from official pages on **6 November 2026**. Programs change often: always confirm on the official page before you start or book. `Unknown` means the fact was not published or could not be confirmed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Prepare honestly. Brain dumps and having someone or something take a course, quiz or exam for you violate every vendor's candidate agreement and lead to revoked credentials and bans.

## Recommended path

| Stage | AWS | Microsoft Azure | Google Cloud | Vendor-neutral and platform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundations | AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) | Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901) | Generative AI Leader | Free credentials from the [tracker](free-certifications/README.md) |
| 2. Cloud architecture | Solutions Architect - Associate (SAA-C03) | Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305) | Professional Cloud Architect | TOGAF Foundation or iSAQB CPSA-F |
| 3. AI specialization | Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01) | AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate (AI-103) | Professional Machine Learning Engineer | NVIDIA NCP-AAI, Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate |
| 4. AI architecture | Solutions Architect - Professional | Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert (AB-100) | Professional Cloud Architect renewal (generative AI case studies) | Claude Certified Architect (partners) |

Pick **one cloud column** and go top to bottom. The [prep course](course/README.md) covers the AI content of stages 1 and 3 and the AI design thinking stage 4 expects.
